Cleaning steps

What happens during carpet cleaning?

The method follows the carpet check, room list and confirmed cleaning scope.

Carpet check

  • Identify the fibre and surface
  • Review visible stains and wear
  • Check access and room condition
  • Discuss priority areas

Surface preparation

  • Remove loose surface debris
  • Inspect marks and traffic paths
  • Prepare the agreed rooms
  • Protect nearby surfaces

Carpet extraction

  • Apply a suitable cleaning solution
  • Agitate areas where required
  • Extract soil and residue
  • Work across the confirmed rooms

Final review

  • Check the cleaned carpet
  • Record the completed work
  • Explain expected drying conditions
  • Send the digital report

Permanent discolouration, fibre damage and some old stains may remain. We assess visible marks before treatment and avoid promising complete removal.

What do Sydney customers ask about carpet cleaning?

How much does carpet cleaning cost in Sydney?

Routine residential and commercial carpet cleaning is quoted after we review the carpet area, fibre type, condition and access. Related NSW rates include from $935 for single-room clean-water drying, $175 for a small patch and $260 for small-bedroom stretching.

What is included in carpet cleaning?

The service includes a carpet check, surface preparation, suitable cleaning treatment, extraction across the confirmed rooms and a final digital report with before-and-after photos.

Can carpet cleaning remove stains?

Carpet cleaning can improve many food, drink, soil and traffic marks. The result depends on the stain, fibre, age, condition and products previously used.

Can carpet cleaning remove pet stains and odours?

It can reduce many pet marks and odours in cleanable carpet fibres. Results depend on the depth of contamination and whether it has reached the underlay.

How long does carpet cleaning take?

Time depends on room count, carpet area, condition, access and treatment. We provide a closer estimate after reviewing your rooms and cleaning requirements.

How long does carpet take to dry after cleaning?

Drying time depends on the fibre, cleaning method, airflow, humidity and room conditions. We explain the expected drying time after inspecting the carpet.

Is steam cleaning safe for carpets?

Yes, steam extraction is suitable for many carpet types when the method matches the fibre and condition. We check the carpet before selecting the treatment.

Can you clean carpets in apartments and commercial sites?

Yes. We can quote suitable carpets in apartments, houses, offices, retail sites, strata areas, clinics, schools and other workplaces. Access, operating hours and site use form part of the scope.
