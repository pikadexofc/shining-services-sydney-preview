Cleaning steps

What happens during upholstery steam cleaning?

The exact method follows the fabric check and the confirmed furniture list.

Fabric check

  • Identify the upholstery type
  • Read available care labels
  • Review worn or damaged areas
  • Check colour stability where needed

Surface preparation

  • Remove loose surface debris
  • Inspect visible spots and marks
  • Prepare accessible cushions
  • Protect the nearby work area

Steam extraction

  • Apply the suitable cleaning solution

  • Extract soil and cleaning residue
  • Work across the confirmed surfaces

Final review

Before you book

What do Sydney customers ask about upholstery cleaning?

How much does upholstery cleaning cost in Sydney?

Our NSW rates start at $40 for a dining or office chair. An armchair costs $75. A two-seater fabric sofa costs $149. A three-seater costs $220. A four-seater costs $290. These prices include GST. We confirm the full furniture list before booking.

Do you clean fabric sofas, couches and other upholstered furniture?

Yes. We clean fabric sofas, couches, modular lounges, armchairs, dining chairs, office chairs, chaises and ottomans. The price follows the furniture type or seat count. Mattresses and leather furniture have their own cleaning services.

What is included in upholstery cleaning?

The service includes a fabric assessment and surface preparation. We treat suitable visible marks and clean the confirmed upholstery with the selected method. We then check the completed work and send your digital report.

Can you clean all upholstery fabrics safely?

Yes. We assess the upholstery first and select a method that suits the material and its condition. Steam extraction is used when it is safe for the fabric. This fabric check helps protect the colour, texture and backing during cleaning.

Can upholstery cleaning remove stains?

Yes. Cleaning can remove or reduce many food, drink and everyday marks. The expected result depends on the substance, fabric condition, stain age and any products previously applied. We assess visible stains before choosing the treatment.

Can upholstery cleaning remove pet stains and odours?

Yes. We can treat many pet stains and reduce odours held within cleanable upholstery fibres. The result depends on the severity and depth of the contamination. Odour removal may be limited when urine has reached the cushion filling or internal frame.

How long does upholstery cleaning take?

Most upholstery appointments take around 1 to 3 hours. A single small sofa may take less time. Several pieces or detailed stain treatment may take longer. We confirm a closer estimate after reviewing the item count, size and condition.

How long does furniture take to dry after upholstery cleaning?

Most steam-cleaned furniture dries in around 4 to 6 hours. Fabric type, airflow, humidity and room conditions can change this estimate. We explain the expected drying time after cleaning. Wait until the upholstery is fully dry before using it.
