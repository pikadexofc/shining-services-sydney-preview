Sydney properties

What changes when we clean windows in Sydney?

Sydney properties range from apartments and terraces to offices, shopfronts and strata buildings. Each site has different glass, access and scheduling needs.

  • Apartments may need lift, concierge or loading access
  • Terraces and townhouses can add stairs and difficult windows
  • Shopfronts need timing that suits customers and staff
  • Parking instructions help the cleaner arrive with equipment

Weather, salt air and traffic can leave marks on exterior glass. We confirm the glass condition and safe access before selecting the cleaning scope.

Window cleaning questions

What do Sydney customers usually ask before booking?

How much does window cleaning cost in Sydney?

Published NSW home rates start at $170 for up to 10 windows. A medium home is $249. A large home with 20 or more windows is $369. Double-storey or difficult access starts at $435. Guide rates are $20 per window for inside and outside cleaning and $15 per window for track detail. Prices include GST.

What is included in a standard window clean?

A standard window clean covers suitable glass areas confirmed in your quote. It can include interior and exterior glass, reachable frames, sills, doors and tracks. Commercial properties receive a site-based checklist.

Can you clean windows inside and outside?

Yes. Inside and outside glass can be included where access is safe and suitable. We confirm the window count, height, access method and surface condition before the visit.

Can you clean double-storey windows?

Yes, double-storey or difficult-access windows can be quoted from the published NSW starting rate of $435. We confirm safe access and the exact glass areas first.

How often should windows be professionally cleaned?

The right schedule depends on weather exposure, traffic, building use and presentation needs. Many customers choose a seasonal, quarterly or one-off visit.

Can you clean shopfronts, offices and strata windows?

Yes. We clean suitable shopfronts, offices, showrooms, strata common areas and managed properties. Commercial sites need a walkthrough because access and scope vary.

Can I track the window cleaning visit?

Yes. Our customer tracking system shows the cleaner's visit steps and timing. You can follow the booking without waiting at the property.

What happens after the window cleaning visit?

We send a digital report with before-and-after photos. You can leave feedback through the report. If agreed work needs attention, we fix it until you are satisfied.
