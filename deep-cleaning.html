Service scope

What does a deep cleaning service cover?

A deep clean tackles built-up dust and grime across several rooms. It gives the cleaner more time for edges, fixtures and missed areas.

We set the checklist around your home's condition. Name the rooms and surfaces that need the closest attention.

This is not an end of lease clean or a specialist treatment. Carpet steam cleaning, oven interiors, exterior windows and mould work are separate unless your quote includes them.

Sydney homes

What should we know before deep cleaning a Sydney home?

Sydney homes range from CBD apartments to terraces and larger family houses. Each layout changes the access, equipment setup and time needed for detailed work.

  • For apartments, share lift bookings and concierge rules before arrival
  • Tell us about narrow stairs or limited access in terraces and townhouses
  • Larger homes need clear priorities when several rooms have build-up
  • Nearby parking matters because the team brings the cleaning equipment

Bathrooms and laundries may hold moisture when ventilation is poor. We can clean suitable visible surfaces within the quote. Cleaning cannot repair leaks or remove hidden mould.

What is the difference between house cleaning and deep cleaning?

House cleaning covers routine upkeep. Deep cleaning suits heavier build-up and areas that need more detail. We will suggest the right service after you describe the home's condition.
